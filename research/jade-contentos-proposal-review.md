## 1. What the proposal actually says (verbatim substance)

**Credibility claim:** "based on the work I did for Plot Joinery, which grew their audience from 15K → 140K+ followers and generated over 7-figures in content-attributed *quotes* (not just leads) in our first 12 months."

- That is his stated future price for the programme.
- **Intro price: $7,000/mo, 3-month commitment.** ("Limited to 3 spots.")

## 4. Australian market benchmarks (what $7k/mo buys elsewhere)

**Social media management retainers (AU, 2026):**
- Small business, organic, ~2 platforms, basic strategy + reporting: **$1,000–$2,000/mo**
- Typical AU SMB agency retainer band: **$2,000–$14,500/mo**
- Full-service (strategy + content + ads + reporting): **$3,000–$8,000+/mo**
- Extra platforms beyond two: ~$300–600/mo each

**Video / content retainers (AU, 2026):**
- Solo videographer: **$750–$2,000/mo**
- Full-service agency w/ editors, designers, strategists: **$5,000+/mo**
- Standard $5,000–$8,000/mo packages typically include **~2 shoot days + multiple edits + strategy calls**
- Mid-market agencies: $2,000–$5,000/mo for **12–16 posts/mo**
- High-end AU video retainers: $10,000–$35,000/mo for 3–8 high-production videos
- UGC-style creator retainers: $2,500–$6,000/mo for 8–12 deliverables

**In-house alternative (AU salaries, 2026):**
- Content Creator: **$71k–$85k**/yr (~$6–7k/mo + super + gear + management)
- Videographer: **$85k–$95k**/yr (Sydney ~$96k; senior 8+ yrs ~$111k)
- Video Editor: **$70k–$85k**/yr

**Construction-niche AU players found:** Legacy Media (Melbourne — progress reels, vertical video, photography, timelapse, drone; monthly retainer = *8–12 posts + one shoot day + edit bundle*), The Creative Collective, Melotti Content Media, VEX Management, Content Builders.

**Headline read:** **$7,000/mo sits at the top of the "full-service agency" band** — and Jade's offer does **not** include filming, paid ads, or a stated deliverable volume. At $12,000/mo it would be priced above most AU full-service construction-marketing agencies. The premium is being charged for **strategy + coaching + editing + the Plot case study**, not for production volume.
