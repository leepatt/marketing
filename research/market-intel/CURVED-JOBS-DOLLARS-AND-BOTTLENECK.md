# Curved Jobs — Dollar Sizing & the Quoting Bottleneck

_Update to `CURVED-JOBS-WINLOSS.md` + `VOICE-OF-CUSTOMER-curved-jobs.md`. From a $-sizing pass over ~24
more won/lost jobs (Quotient quote totals + ClickUp enquiries), 2026-07-21._

## The headline finding: the leak is our quoting step, not the market

On **11 of 12 stalled ("awaiting approval") jobs, the ClickUp "Quote" field was EMPTY** — no quote was ever
linked to the task. Most stalled at *"plans received → awaiting our quote,"* **not** at customer rejection.

- So "awaiting approval" ≠ "the customer said no." For much of the stalled pile it means **a quote never
  went out (or wasn't logged).** Either way, the stall is at **our** step.
- This reframes the 50% formwork "loss rate": a big chunk isn't lost to price or DIY — it's lost to a
  **slow/missing quote.** It matches the customer voice exactly (*"price this asap," "turnaround?," "need
  them asap"*).
- **The #1 conversion lever is internal: quote faster, on every enquiry.** Fixing this likely beats any copy
  tweak. (Caveat: the empty field could mean "not sent" or "not logged in ClickUp" — but either way the
  quoting/follow-through step is where jobs go cold.)

## Expanded voice + nuggets (new, from this batch)

- **Material often pre-specified, or "whatever you normally use."** "Happy to use the 17mm form ply"
  (Oxbuilt); "16 plates cut in whatever size ply you normally use" (AC Building). → Some want the expert
  call, some just want it cut — the offer should make the ply decision effortless either way.
- **"Templates for the chippies to build the walls"** (Outside Ideas) / "we can build on site and add
  bracing" (Butterworth): a chunk of demand is **parts/templates only** — DIY-capable trades who assemble
  themselves. Lower value, more price-shopped.
- **Interstate freight recurs on the bigger jobs** — VL (Brisbane), McCosker (QLD tanks), Desman (NSW).
  We promise Australia-wide shipping; interstate is a real friction on heavy formwork/templates.
- **Competitor sighting:** Northpoint lost the curved **hobs to cast aluminium** — an alternative method we
  compete against (like steel for curved fencing).
- **Feasibility is the emotional hurdle:** "help me get these curves cut **perfectly**" (Khosh), "is this
  something **you can do**" (VL), "able to provide **in a timely manner**" (Earthscape). Reassurance +
  speed converts.
- **Technical/repeat buyers exist:** Desman (engineer, interstate) asks about **bit diameter / tooling** and
  wants the leftover rings — sophisticated, proven ($825 prior job). Hamilton spec'd radii to the mm
  (17.960m / 18.160m), radial-cut ends.
- **Verbatim adds:** "top and bottom plates," "the radius is to the outside of the wall," "ready to
  install," "solid block under the curve," "curved whaler beams," "splice piece for every join."

## Implications (updated)

1. **Fix quoting speed — the biggest lever.** A fast, reliable quote-out process (even a same-day l/m
   ball-park) likely lifts conversion more than any offer/copy change. Consider a rapid-quote SLA + a
   templated l/m rate for common curved-plate jobs.
2. **Offer still matters** for the genuinely price/DIY-sensitive parts-only jobs — lead on speed + finish.
3. **Architraves = low volume, high value** — a $3.6k job exists. Worth a targeted push, not just parking.
4. **Sort interstate freight/lead-time** — it recurs on the bigger-ticket jobs.
