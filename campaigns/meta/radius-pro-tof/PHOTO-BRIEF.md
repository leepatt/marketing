# Radius Pro TOF — Photo / Carousel Brief (what to shoot or request)

> The fuel for the native ads (hack 4: real content). This is the shot list per ad. Phone photos are
> fine — down-and-dirty is the point. Every carousel needs one **product-detail slide** (a cut curved
> panel / the radius edge) — that's the only slide that makes the "it comes cut to size" point.

## The rule of thumb: which to lead with

- **Formwork / process shots** = the scroll-stopper + the craft + they *show our product* (the cut
  ply). Best hook for **concreters & formworkers** (it's their world).
- **Finished-product shots** = the payoff, the aspiration, "it's real and beautiful." Best hook for
  **landscapers, builders & end-clients** (they think in finished results).
- **Both, as a carousel** = strongest. Hook with one, pay off with the other. High dwell, very native.
- Keep slides **clean** — no designed text/logos. The hook + story live in the caption. (If a slide-1
  text hook is wanted, keep it plain/native, like an organic post — never a brand card.)

---

## AD 1 · CONCRETERS — Ardreagh carousel (formwork-led)
1. **The serpentine formwork** (have it) — curiosity hook.
2. **Pour / stripping out** — concrete going in, or forms coming off. The transformation.
3. **Finished garden beds** — poured, planted, in the courtyard. The payoff.
4. **Macro of a cut curved formply panel / the radius edge** — the product money shot.
**To request from the Ardreagh customer:** finished beds photo(s), a pour-or-stripped shot, a close-up
of the cut ply, and a wide shot showing the feature in the apartment courtyard.

## AD 2 · LANDSCAPERS — Ardreagh carousel (result-led)
Same photos as AD1, **reordered**:
1. **Finished garden beds** in the planted courtyard — the aspirational hook.
2. **The formwork** — "here's how the curves were actually made."
3. **Macro of the cut curved panel / radius** — the how.
(Single-image alt: just the finished beds, beauty shot.)

## AD 3 · FORMWORKERS — the formwork + the finish
1. **Cut curved formply panels** (stacked / being set) — "turned up cut to the radius."
2. **Braced formwork** ready to pour — the setup.
3. **Stripped off-form curved concrete** showing a clean finish — the proof it comes up clean.
**Shot to get:** a stripped curved wall/bed showing the off-form finish. Ardreagh formwork works as
interim slide 1–2.

## AD 4 · BUILDERS / CHIPPIES — curved wall plates ⏳ need photos
Ideal carousel:
1. **The cut curved top & bottom plates** — stacked/delivered or laid on the slab. "These turned up cut."
2. **Plates set out on the slab**, following the curve (set-out marks visible).
3. **Studs standing** between the curved plates — the wall going up.
4. **Finished curved wall** sheeted.
**Any one of these as a single is strong** — especially the cut plates on the slab, or the framed curve.

## AD 5 · CURVED BENCH SEATS — need photos ⏳
1. **The curved seat formwork** — the setup / the curve.
2. **Finished curved concrete seat** in use (courtyard/landscape, timber top optional) — the payoff.
3. **Macro of the cut curved panel** — the product point.
**Single alt:** the finished curved bench seat (beauty), or the seat formwork (craft).
