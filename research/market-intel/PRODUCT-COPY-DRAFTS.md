_Direct-response copy for ads + landing pages, built from the **real enquiry language** in
`VOICE-OF-CUSTOMER-curved-jobs.md` + the offer doctrine in `MARKETING-BIBLE.md` §4. Voice = `craftons-voice`
(builder-to-builder, dry, no emoji, no fake urgency). These are **drafts for Lee to approve/sharpen** —
anything using the fit guarantee is gated on Lee approving it. This is ads/DR tone, not social._

> Every line traces to something a customer actually said (quote refs in the VoC doc). The offer's job for
> concreters is to **beat their in-house method** — they can make the formwork themselves, so we lead on
> **speed + finish + no-slog + price certainty**, not "we can cut it."

## The offer (all products) — refined with their words

> **Send us your set-out. We CNC-cut your curved parts to your radius — top and bottom plates, face ply,
> architraves — labelled and ready to fix. Not sure which ply gets the finish? That's our call. Fits your
> line first time, or we re-cut it free.** *(guarantee gated)*

Why it lands (from the VoC):
- *"cut to your set-out," "top and bottom plates," "to your radius"* = their exact words.
- *"which ply gets the finish? That's our call"* answers the most-repeated question (Top Form, Policrete
  both asked us to recommend the ply for a good finish).
- *fit guarantee* answers the DIY-vs-buy hesitation and the "is this achievable?" doubt.
- Pair with **fast turnaround** everywhere — speed decides these jobs ("price this asap… ready Friday?").

- Your curved walls, plated and ready to frame. Tails cut to suit on site.

## 2. Curved Formwork & Seats — the Formwork Builder (audience: concreters / formworkers)

_This is the 50%-conversion product. They can build the formwork themselves — so the copy must beat their
own method on **speed, finish, and the slog**, and own the ply-for-finish question they keep asking._

**Hero:** *Stop hand-building your curved formwork. We CNC-cut your top and bottom plates and face ply to
your radius — a perfect curve, ready to pour.*

**Headlines (U-U-S-U):**
- Bending form ply around a radius by hand is a slog. Send the set-out — we cut it to your line.
- Curved bench seat? We cut the formwork to your radius — priced by the lineal metre.
- Which ply gets a clean off-form finish? That's our call. Send the radius, we'll spec it and cut it.
- Perfect curve, every time — CNC-cut to your set-out, not freehand around a stringline.

**Lead paragraph (landing/ad):**
> Pricing a curved seat or an insitu curved wall? Send your set-out and we'll CNC-cut the top and bottom
> plates and face ply to your radius — the exact curve, ready to assemble and pour. We'll tell you which ply
> and thickness gets the finish you're after (that's the bit most stuff up). Priced by the lineal metre,
> turned around fast. Beats a day bending ply and hoping it holds the line.

**Answers their questions:** *l/m rate?* → Yes, priced per lineal metre. *What ply for the finish?* → We
recommend and supply it. *Achievable?* → Send the radius, we'll confirm and quote.

_Tiny volume today, but a clean niche with a competitor charging a per-arc setup fee._

**Headlines (U-U-S-U):**

- Curved architraves, cut to your profile. Ball-park price back fast.
- Send the arch, get it cut. Curved and straight architraves, matched to your design.

**Lead paragraph (landing/ad):**
> Curved or arched architraves are the fiddly bit — bending and laminating them by hand rarely comes up
> clean. Send us your drawing and the profile and we'll cut them to your radius in MDF or solid timber,
> matched to your design. No per-arc setup fee. Straight lengths in the same order, sorted.

**Answers their questions:** *Ball-park cost inc material?* → Yes. *Match my profile/design?* → Yes, send
the drawing. *Turnaround?* → ~2 weeks (per a real quote).

## Cross-cutting rules (from the VoC)

- **Keep the phone in it.** These jobs close by conversation — every CTA should make it easy to send a plan
  *and* call. The online builder / instant price is the *soft entry*, not the whole funnel.
- **Kill the file friction in the CTA:** "Send a PDF, DXF, or even a marked-up photo of your plan." (Their
  #1 friction was "screenshots won't work.")
- **Lead with speed.** State a real turnaround. Slow quoting loses these jobs.
- **Be the expert.** Every product answers "which ply/thickness for the finish" — that's what earns trust.
