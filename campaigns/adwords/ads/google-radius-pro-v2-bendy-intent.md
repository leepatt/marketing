# Google RSA — Radius Pro v2 (bendy-ply intent)

## Why v1 failed

Hard numbers, 1 Jul – 2 Aug: the Radius Pro ad group spent **$555.44 over 97 clicks and produced
zero conversions.** Quality Scores across its keywords are **1–5**, mostly 1.

Two causes, both visible in the account:

**1. The two live ads were byte-identical.** Same 15 headlines, same 4 descriptions. The only
difference was a pin on HEADLINE_1 — which is exactly what dropped that ad to **POOR** ad strength
(pinning kills asset variety). There was no A/B test happening; it was the same ad twice.

**2. Not one asset used the words people actually type.** The live copy says *Curved Plywood*,
*Radius Pro*, *Curves*. Here is what searchers actually typed:

| Search term | Spend | Conv |
|---|---|---|
| bendable plywood | $29.51 | 0 |
| flexible plywood | $23.90 | 0 |
| curved mdf panels | $17.32 | 0 |
| bendy plywood / bendy ply / bendable ply | $17.76 | 0 |
| flexible mdf | $5.47 | 0 |
| bending plywood near me | $6.00 | 0 |

The words **bendy**, **bendable**, **flexible**, **bending** and **MDF** appear **zero times** across
all 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Google reads that as a weak match and charges accordingly —
that's the QS 1 and the $5.90 CPC in one sentence.

## The angle

These searchers are solution-aware but **aware of the wrong solution**. They're planning to buy a
flat flexible sheet and bend it themselves — kerf cuts, laminating, springback, swearing.

Don't fight the term. **Meet it, then reframe.** Their problem isn't "I need bendy ply", it's "I need
a curve". Bendy ply is just the only way they knew how to get one.

> The pitch: you were going to bend it yourself. Don't. We'll cut the curve.

This is the reframe `brand/keyword-plan.md` specified from the start — it just never made it into the
ad copy or onto the page.

## Pin nothing

v1's POOR rating came from pinning HEADLINE_1. Leave every asset unpinned and let Google mix. If the
product name must lead, that's what the display path is for.

## Second ad — make it genuinely different

Running two identical ads taught the account nothing for a month. The second ad in this group
(814855235968, AVERAGE) should stay live **as the control** until v2 has ~100 clicks, then be replaced
with a real variant. Suggested axis for variant B: lead on **speed** ("Dispatched In 3 Days") rather
than **the bending reframe**, so the test answers a real question.

## Voice check

Against `craftons-voice`: outcome-first, fragments for punch, no questions in headlines, specific
timelines ("3 days"), Australian-made as a quiet trust signal, no banned words (no *streamline*,
*premium*, *solutions*, *revolutionary*). "No Bending. No Springback." follows the locked three-beat
negative pattern of "No on-site cutting. No improvising."

## Rip Pro — resolved

Shopify shows a `Craftons Rip Pro: Bendy Formply — Cut to Size` product, which raised the question of
whether bendy-ply searchers should be routed there instead. **Lee's call (2026-08-03): Rip Pro is not
advertised.** Ads run for Radius Pro, Architrave Builder and Formwork Builder only.

So the reframe in this ad stands as written — bendy-ply traffic goes to Radius Pro, and the copy meets
the search term before redirecting the intent. No cross-link to Rip Pro on the landing page.
