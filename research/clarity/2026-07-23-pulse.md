# Clarity behaviour pulse — craftons.com.au (2026-07-23)

_First pull, the day Clarity went live. Source: Microsoft Clarity Data Export API
(`project-live-insights`), **last 3 days**. **Sample is small and fresh — treat page-level
numbers as directional, not settled.** The device-level split (n≈1,184 sessions) is the robust
signal; per-page friction rates (n = 3–40) are noisy._

---

## Headline: the site is a mobile shopfront, and mobile is leaking

92% of sessions are on a phone — and on a phone people barely engage before leaving. Desktop is a
small minority but behaves like a genuinely interested buyer. The paid traffic is landing on mobile;
the money is being left on mobile.

| Metric | 📱 Mobile | 💻 PC | Tablet |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sessions (3d)** | **1,091 (92%)** | 81 (7%) | 12 |
| Avg scroll depth | **19%** | 51% | 20% |
| Pages / session | **1.14** | 2.11 | 1.15 |
| Active time / session | **19s** | 118s | 9s |

Read that bottom row again: **desktop visitors are ~6× more engaged than mobile.** Mobile users see
one page, scroll a fifth of the way down, and are gone in ~19 seconds. That pattern — huge mobile
volume + near-zero mobile engagement — is the single biggest thing to fix, and it's almost certainly
costing configurator starts and quote requests.

Mobile also throws **JS script errors in 2.8% of sessions (49 hits)** while desktop throws none — a
mobile-specific technical fault could be part of why the phone experience underperforms. Worth a look
in recordings.

### Friction worth watching (verify in the dashboard before acting)

1. **`/pages/contact` — 23% dead-clicks + 23% quick-backs.** This is a money page (the quote/contact
   path). Nearly a quarter of visitors click something that doesn't respond. If real, this is directly
   costing leads. **Watch 3–4 recordings of this page first.** Note STATUS still lists the real
   quote-page URL as an open question — this data suggests confirming and pressure-testing it.
2. **`/products/radius-online` — 138s active but only 34% scroll + 13% quick-back.** Good news: people
   who reach the Radius configurator genuinely engage (longest active time on site). The scroll/quick-back
   hints the configurator sits low on mobile — worth checking it's above the fold on a phone.
3. **`/products/part-builder-online` — 100% scroll but 7s active.** People blow through to the bottom
   and leave almost instantly. Reads like a thin or confusing page, not a considered one.
4. **`/products/craftons-radius-pro-plywood` — 13% scroll, 4s active.** Radius Pro is a *confirmed
   converter* from the keyword work, yet this page gets an instant bounce. Small n (5), but flag it.
5. **`/collections/all` is the #1 landing page (40 sess) with 20% quick-back.** A lot of people start on
   "all products" and bounce — a merchandising/navigation signal (are ads pointing here instead of at a
   specific product?).

## What the API can and can't tell us (so we use the right tool)

- **This report = aggregate signal** (the API's job): *where* the friction and drop-off concentrate.
- **The "why" = the Clarity dashboard**: for each flagged page above, watch the **session recordings**
  and open the **click/scroll heatmaps**. The API points the torch; the dashboard shows the face.
- API limits: 10 calls/project/day, last 1–3 days only, ≤3 dimensions/call — this pulse uses 2 calls
  (by Device, by URL).

---

## Recommended next moves

1. **Mobile-first audit.** The 92%/6×-gap is the headline. Pull 5–10 mobile recordings (esp. on the
   configurator pages) and check for the 2.8% script errors. This is where growth is hiding.
2. **Fix the contact/quote path** if recordings confirm the dead-clicks.
3. **Give it a week**, then re-pulse for a stable baseline (per-page n is too small today). Once there's
   ~7 days of data, a visual behaviour dashboard is worth building.
4. **Feed it back to ads:** if `/collections/all` is a paid landing page, point ads at specific product
   configurators instead.

## How this was pulled (reproducible)

```
GET https://www.clarity.ms/export-data/api/v1/project-live-insights?numOfDays=3&dimension1=Device
GET https://www.clarity.ms/export-data/api/v1/project-live-insights?numOfDays=3&dimension1=URL
Header: Authorization: Bearer $CLARITY_API_TOKEN
```
Gotchas: the URL dimension returns the page under the key **`Url`** (not `URL`), caps at **1,000 rows**,
and includes non-site noise (`https://Electron`, `Url:null` aggregate) — filter to `craftons.com.au`.
Metrics returned: Traffic, EngagementTime, ScrollDepth, plus DeadClick / RageClick / Quickback /
ExcessiveScroll / ScriptError / ErrorClick counts.
