## 2. Fire obligations in the lease (REIV Code 143)

**Outgoings payable by the tenant — cl 2.1:**
- **(g)** fire service levies
- **(h)** insurance premiums, *"including (but not limited to) fire insurance on all improvements
  on a replacement and reinstatement basis"*, plus plant/equipment breakdown, public liability
  (**$20,000,000** per the Schedule), and loss-of-rent/consequential loss cover
- **(i)** *"the expense of the attendance of the fire brigade in response to a fire alarm generated
  from or in connection with the premises"* — **false-alarm callout charges land on the tenant**
- **(j)** inspecting, auditing, servicing, repairing, maintaining, monitoring and testing **all
  essential safety measures**

**Building operating expenses — cl 3.1:** tenant pays a proportionate share of **fire protection**
and **essential safety measures**.

**Tenant's own insurance — cl 4.4:** must keep current a policy covering the tenant's and others'
goods for reinstatement/replacement cost against damage or destruction by **fire**, water, theft,
malicious and accidental damage, storm, lightning and tempest, earthquake, explosion, impact by
vehicles/aircraft and articles dropped from aircraft.

**Repair obligation — cl 5.1:** tenant repairs and maintains, but damage *"by fire or other cause not
attributable to the default or negligence of the tenant"* is **excepted**.

**Notice of defects — cl 7.1(b)(v):** tenant must promptly give written notice of any accident or
defect affecting **essential safety fixtures and fittings** connected to or serving the premises.

**Damage and destruction — cl 27:**
- **27.1** If the premises are wholly or partly damaged or destroyed **by any cause** so as to be
  unfit for use, and the cause was not the tenant's default, and the landlord's insurance has not
  been vitiated by the tenant — then rent and outgoings (or a fair proportion) **are suspended**
  for as long as the premises are unfit.
- **27.2** Disputes about the proportion or period of abatement go to arbitration under the
  Commercial Arbitration Act 2011; abatement is in full satisfaction of all the tenant's claims.
- **27.3** If the premises are **totally or substantially destroyed and not reinstated within
  3 months**, either party may **terminate by written notice**.

**Cessation of services — cl 28:** no damages, compensation or rent abatement for failure or
breakdown of services — and **"services" expressly includes sprinklers and alarms**.

**Indemnity — cl 30:** landlord not liable for damage/injury not attributable to its own
negligence; tenant indemnifies the landlord for claims arising from the tenant's use.

**Definitions — cl 39:** *"essential safety measure"* takes its meaning from the Act.

## Discrepancies and open questions

1. **Two versions of the same disclosure statement.** Both are dated 23 Aug 2023 at item 20.4,
   but the 30 Aug scan has the amounts column completed and the 23 Aug scan does not.
   Three fire-relevant figures changed:
   - Fire services property levy: blank → **$105**
   - Insurance: **$2,300 approx → $1,900 approx** (plus an added note,
     "LAND TAX NOT INCLUDED AS IT IS A RETAIL LEASE")
   - Essential safety measures/audits: **$500 → $105**
   Worth confirming which version was the one actually served on the tenant, since the Retail
   Leases Act gives the tenant remedies if a disclosure statement is misleading or incomplete.

2. **$105 appears twice** in the 30 Aug version — as both the fire services property levy (13.6)
   and the essential safety measures line (13.10). Given 13.10 was $500 in the earlier scan,
   this looks like it may be a transcription slip rather than a genuine revision.

3. **The outgoings don't reconcile.** 30 Aug itemised: $3,600 + $1,200 + $105 + $1,900 + $105
   = **$6,910**, against a stated estimate of **$6,800** at 13.11. The 23 Aug figures reconcile
   even less well ($7,600+ before the levy).

4. **"FES SERVICES $190 PER INSPECTION" has no stated frequency.** At quarterly inspections
   that is $760/yr, which is far above the $105 (or $500) disclosed for essential safety measures
   at 13.10. Frequency should be pinned down — it materially changes the outgoings estimate.

5. **The lease document is watermarked "Sample Copy Only"** and the Schedule's execution page
   (doc006137 p3) is **unsigned** — no landlord signature, no tenant director signature, no
   witnesses. This pack appears to be the pre-signing disclosure set, not an executed lease.

6. **No sprinklers disclosed**, yet cl 28 disclaims liability for sprinkler failure and the
   fitout list at 1.4 leaves the box unticked. Confirm what fire protection actually exists
   on site before agreeing to the essential safety measures maintenance obligation at cl 2.1(j).
