# The Craftons Marketing Checklist

_The ordered, phased action list to execute `MARKETING-BIBLE.md`. Check items off as they're done so we
never repeat work (same discipline as `STATUS.md`). Owner tags: **[Lee]** decision/approval,
**[Claude]** engine can build/draft, **[Tia]** shoot/production, **[Ops]** business/delivery side._

> before scaling spend. Don't jump to "scale ads" while the missing middle (capture) is still empty.
> **⭐ = highest leverage / do first.** 🚧 = gated on a Lee decision or another item.

      **pains · desires · objections · exact language · trigger events.**
- [ ] **[Claude] Mine the sewers** and drop verbatim quotes in (no paraphrasing):
  - [ ] Reddit — r/AusConstruction, r/carpentry, r/Formwork1, r/woodworking (curved/bending threads).
  - [ ] AU Facebook trade groups (carpentry / formwork / shopfitting).
  - [ ] Comments on our posts + on competitor/benchmark accounts.
  - [ ] Competitor **one-star reviews** (their failures = our positioning).
- [ ] ⭐ **[Claude/Ops] Mine our own first-party goldmine** — the 443 tracked lead-form submissions,
      call notes, configurator sessions, email replies. This is intel competitors can't see.

      deadline, what they've tried before*. (Feeds intel **and** lead quality.)

      the market's own words for the problem before copy is written. No quote = not ready to write.
- [ ] **[Claude] Schedule a recurring intel refresh** (routine) — markets move; keep it living, not once-off.

## PHASE 2 — Pillar Two: build the missing middle (capture the 97%) ⭐

This is our biggest gap. Right now social builds brand and AdWords harvests the 3%, with nothing between.

### 2a. Soft offer #1 — market what we already own (fast win)

- [ ] ⭐ **[Claude] Reframe the configurators as the soft offer** — lead with **"design it & get instant
      pricing online — no sales call"** on ads and product pages, *instead of* "get a quote." (Sabri's
      less-threatening-offer principle; we already have the mechanism built.)
- [ ] **[Claude] Rewrite the AdWords landing pages** to lead with instant-pricing + the Godfather offer
      + risk reversal (once the offer is approved). Send paid clicks here, never the homepage.

### 2b. Soft offer #2 — the lead magnet (build the capture)

- [ ] ⭐ **[Claude] Create the high-value content offer: "The Curved Work Field Guide"** — min bending
      radii by thickness · setting out a curved wall without cracks · bending ply vs kerf vs laminate ·
      spec'ing off-form that passes. Maps onto validated keyword pillars (`keyword-plan.md`).
- [ ] **[Lee] Approve the Field Guide** before it goes live (human-approves-every-asset rule).
- [ ] **[Claude/Ops] Build the capture page + delivery** — name/email/phone → instant PDF to inbox.
- [ ] **[Ops] Confirm email tool / list** for capture + nurture (see `INTEGRATIONS.md`).

### 2c. Nurture — turn captured leads into jobs

- [ ] **[Claude] Write the email nurture sequence** (use the `email-sequences` skill) — educate with
      more value (the craft), soft-bridge to the configurator/offer when the job lands. Not a sales blast.
- [ ] **[Claude] Set up retargeting** — anyone who watched/read but didn't convert → served the soft
      offer again. (Stitches the funnel together.)
