# Competitors & benchmarks — Craftons

_Detail + receipts in `../inspiration/SWIPE-FILE.md`. Use these as SERP/positioning seeds._

## Building-industry players (direct/adjacent — same audience)
- **Big River Group** (bigrivergroup.com.au) — AU building products manufacturer; **makes plywood +
  formply** → closest category overlap. (Teardown in progress.)
- **Bowens** (bowens.com.au) — VIC family-owned timber & building supplies merchant; same tradie audience.
- **Bunnings** (bunnings.com.au) — giant AU hardware retailer; mass DIY/how-to content machine.

> Craftons' edge vs these: none of them own the **value-first, craft, curved-specialty** lane, and
> none have a **Formwork Builder configurator**. That's the white space.

## Aspirational benchmarks (studied for format/voice, not competitors)
- **Gozney** — lifestyle-led; product incidental; "How To Pizza" education-as-a-show.
- **July** — premium DTC; "one feature, one shot"; restraint = premium.
- **Bellroy** — make a functional product desirable; demo = sell; "show the invisible".
- **Hommey** — community machine ("by you"); translate mechanics not the cutesy voice.
- **BuildPass** — same builder audience; cautionary (inside-out feed → dead engagement).
- **The B1M** — cinematic construction storytelling; own the diagram/story spine.

## Proprietary advantage (for the "can we win" test)
Craftons actually **manufactures** curved ply / formwork and runs a **world-first Formwork Builder**
configurator (curved bench seats, curved walls, firepits). Real how-to expertise + real CAD/CNC.
Prioritize keywords where this unique angle wins over generic suppliers.
