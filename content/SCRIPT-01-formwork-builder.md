# Script 01 — Formwork Builder launch

**Lee's words, final.** ~350 characters, roughly 30 seconds. Workshop only.

---

## The script

> "We've been getting countless enquiries from concreters since word spread about the Radius Pro.
> They all want the formwork for the whole project to be cut, not just the curves.
>
> So we built the Formwork Builder. Put in your shape, your radius, the height of the structure, and
> it works out the plates and prices it on the spot.
>
> We then cut the whole lot and send it to site with a setout plan."

**Stop there.** No sign-off, no logo, no "link in bio".

**Caption:** The whole job, not just the curves.

---

## Why this version works

**"The whole project, not just the curves"** is the line the whole video rests on. It's the actual
reason the Formwork Builder exists, it's specific, and it's the thing a concreter recognises
immediately. Everything else is in service of it.

**The setout plan** at the end is the thing that makes the pack usable, and it closes on a
deliverable rather than a claim.

---

## Visuals

| You say | On screen |
|---|---|
| "enquiries from concreters… Radius Pro" | **Plate edge, tight.** Ply laminations and film face filling frame, tracking slowly along the curve |
| "the whole project, not just the curves" | **Straights and curves together.** A stack with both in it, or a set-out drawing of a whole structure. This shot has to show *more than a curve* |
| "So we built the Formwork Builder" | **You.** Bench, machine behind, natural light. First and only time |
| "your shape, your radius, the height" | **Screen.** Wall Shape row, then Inner Radius, then Height |
| "works out the plates and prices it on the spot" | **Screen.** The curve splitting into plates, then the price |
| "cut the whole lot and send it to site" | **The stack**, wider than the opening. Workshop context |
| "with a setout plan" | **A printed setout sheet**, phone straight down over it on the bench |

**Don't open on your face.** Not one of Plot's top performers does. You arrive at "so we built the
Formwork Builder", which is the problem-to-solution turn.

The screen footage will look nothing like the phone footage — clean and white against a warm
workshop. That contrast is the point. Don't grade them to match.

---

## Have ready

- A clean stack of plates, with straights in it as well as curves
- A printed setout plan
- A real radius from a real job to type in
- Browser with the chrome hidden. Record at 1440 wide, crop to a tall slice of the left panel in the
  edit — shape buttons, radius field and readout all stack vertically there

---

## One note on length

350 characters sits under the 300–600 band (median 166 likes on Plot's data) rather than the 600–900
band that runs 275. If a fourth sentence turns up naturally when you say it out loud, keep it.

Not padding it to hit a number. A tight video that gets shot beats a longer one that doesn't.
