## The one detail

**Every plate comes out with its own ID cut into it.** That's the whole video.

It qualifies on all three counts the skill cares about: it's Systems content (§2.3), which Plot
structurally cannot make and no competitor can copy; it's physical, so it lands on the conveyor
footage you can actually get; and it has a real reason behind it, which is the single
highest-leverage thing in the dataset.

---

**Mechanism, with real numbers** *(§2b beat 2 — specificity is the credibility)*

**Benefit — cost, variation, programme** *(§3 Part III, §9.4)*

> "So when it lands, nobody's guessing. No measuring, no cutting on site, and no plate braced in

> booked."

## What this unlocks

Per §1, one job is eight videos, not one. The same pack gives you: why the curve splits where it
does, why the plates fit a sheet, why the shutters are a different material to the plates, what the
setout saves. Each one its own video, each one its own reason. **Do not combine them.**
