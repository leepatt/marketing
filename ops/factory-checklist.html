The two-week window

The EcoKit LVL job is roughly two weeks off, and those LVLs get cut on the panel saw. So the saw has to be relocated, powered and cutting, with a clear sheet path to it, before the job lands — and it can't be sold until after. Work backwards: layout map → clear out → move the saw → dust extraction → racking.
