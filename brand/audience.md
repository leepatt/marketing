# Audience — Craftons

## Primary: builders / tradies (on the tools)
- Carpenters, formworkers, shopfitters, cabinetmakers building curved/complex work.
- **Sophistication: high.** They know trade terms, NCC, set-out, tolerances. Don't explain basics.
- **Pain points:** curved/radius work is slow, wasteful, and inconsistent done on site (bog-and-sand,
  kerf-cutting, laminating sheets); hard to quote; cracks at the join; tight deadlines.
- **What they want:** parts that turn up cut to their set-out, ready to fix; less rework; faster quotes.
- **Language they search:** "curved plywood", "bending ply", "formply", "how to build a curved wall",
  "off-form concrete", "radius set out", "curved bench seat".

## Secondary: architects / specifiers
- Spec products into drawings; care about finish, compliance, detailing, what's achievable.
- **Pain points:** don't know what curved forms are buildable/affordable; detailing curves; finding a
  supplier who can deliver to spec.
- **What they want:** confidence a curve can be built precisely and on budget; a tool to design it.
- **Language they search:** "curved timber feature wall", "off-form concrete finish", "formwork for
  curved walls", "curved joinery".
