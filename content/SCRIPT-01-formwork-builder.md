## The thing that governs everything

**No talking head, and the video does not open on a face.** Across their top performers, not one opens on
a presenter. In Format A he never faces camera at all — profile, working, hands and a voice. In Format B
the presenter arrives at the **pivot**, roughly a third in, on the line that turns problem into solution.

Shot grammar alternates **wide → extreme close-up → wide**, every 2–4 seconds. Handheld, follows the
action. Lapel mic recorded in situ, not voiceover added later. Burned-in captions, one short phrase at a
time, key spec highlighted. No intro, no outro, no logo.

## Shoot this one first instead

**Format C — the silent trailer.** One slow continuous move across a finished curved piece. Logo top-left,
"CASE STUDY" top-right, serif lower-third title: *"How we build curved formwork →"*. Ten seconds. No
speech, no captions, no script.

On their numbers, silent trailers run a **median 449 likes against 223 for spoken posts**, carry the
highest comment counts in the account (748, 212, 146), and are the cheapest thing in the whole system to
make. It needs one beautiful shot and a title.

It's also the format that asks nothing of Jake's diary, and it can be shot this week.
