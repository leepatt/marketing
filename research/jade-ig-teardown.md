## 2. What the stale data got wrong, and why it matters

The search snapshots described an earlier incarnation: **live music / concert photography**,
143 posts, ~11K followers, a Skool community called **CONCERT PHOTO CLUB** (`skool.com/jade`,
~1,800 members), online courses, and Meta ads to that funnel. Venues cited were Forum Melbourne
and John Cain Arena. A claim of **0 → 11K followers and 3.8M views in 90 days** attached to
that account.

The live account is **30 posts, 9,183 followers, positioned entirely around construction
content, linking to a different Skool community ("Creators Circle")**.

So the photography history was real — it's just **not what the account is now**. Somewhere
between then and now, the account was **repositioned**: post count fell from ~143 to 30, and
the niche, bio and linked product all changed.

I can't tell from a screenshot whether the old posts were deleted or archived. Either way the
repositioning is recent and fairly complete.

## 4. The conclusion that actually changed

**His 9,183 followers are not evidence that he can build a construction audience.**

That audience was acquired in the **concert-photography era** — it is, in the main, an audience
of photographers and music fans. The account has since been repositioned to construction
content with only **30 posts** behind the new direction.

This cuts two ways, and both matter:

**Against him.** The transferability concern I raised earlier is now sharper, not softer. Both
of his demonstrated growth results — concert photography, and Plot's architectural joinery —
are **visually spectacular, broad-appeal content for consumer or design-minded audiences**.
Craftons is narrow B2B trade, technical subject, infrequent unplanned purchase. And his *own*
construction-positioned account has 30 posts and a follower base inherited from a different
niche. **There is still no evidence of this skill working in a narrow technical B2B niche** —
his own account is currently the experiment, not the proof.

**A note on the follower count.** 9,183 now versus ~11K in the older snapshot suggests
attrition, which is exactly what you'd expect when an account repositions away from the niche
its audience joined for. That's normal and not damning — but it means the headline number is
drifting down, not up, on the account he's using as a shopfront.

**For him.** He is demonstrably willing to do the thing he's selling: pick a niche, reposition,
and build. And he did grow a real audience once from nothing.

## 5. What this does to the commercial read

Unchanged, and if anything reinforced. Jade is fundamentally an **info-product and community
operator** — he has now run this playbook twice (Concert Photo Club, then Creators Circle), and
ContentOS is the high-ticket tier of the same product line.

Re-read the proposal's structure with that in view:

| Element in the proposal | What it is |
|---|---|
| "Plot… **$12,000/mo**" + "what I'll ask in future" | Price anchor |
| "will start at **$7,000/mo**" | Intro discount against the anchor |
| "selfishly, to get great case studies" | Discount justification |
| "**Limited to 3 spots**" | Scarcity |
| 3-month commitment | Lock-in |

Textbook high-ticket launch, competently executed. **The $7k and $12k are positioning numbers,
not cost-derived ones — which means they are soft.** The reduced-scope counter-offer (pay for
strategy and workflow build as a one-off, skip the retainer) should land.
