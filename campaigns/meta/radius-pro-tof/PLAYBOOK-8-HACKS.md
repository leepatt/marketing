# The 8 Hacks → how we're running the Radius Pro TOF campaign

> Straight from the source video (Sabri Suby, "$300M in Meta ads, 8 hacks"). Each hack, then the
> exact Craftons move. This governs the 5 ads in `AD-CONCEPTS.md` and the ramp in `LAUNCH-GUIDE.md`.

| # | The hack | What it really says | Our move |
|---|----------|---------------------|----------|
| **1** | **Statics on volume** | Andromeda is a "hungry panda". Needs a flood of fresh creative or ads fatigue. Statics are cheaper/faster and the algo shows *more* of them per session. "Down and dirty native statics" beat 2.5-month polished VSSLs. | **Real job photos as statics.** 1 hr/week cutting fresh ones. **Stop over-designing**. Raw & native wins. |
| **2** | **The one-keyword (identity trigger)** | Andromeda reads your image/copy/offer/page to find the buyer. To reach a niche, put the niche word IN the creative + copy. Duplicate the winner, swap the identity word ("dental leads" → "lawyer leads"). Drops CPL, opens new audience pockets. **"You are NOT selling your product"**. Just funnel applicable people to the page. | **Our niches are the trades + job-types.** 5 ads = 5 identity triggers: **Concreters, Landscapers, Formworkers, Builders**, + job-type **curved bench seats / garden beds / wall plates**. Same winning creative, swap the trigger. |
| **3** | **Winning-format clone** | Don't ride one winner till it dies. Feed the winner to AI → hundreds of variations (body → headlines → creative). CBO lets Meta pivot spend. "Zombie campaign": relaunch the high-conviction no-spend ads → ~20% more winners. | Once one ad wins, **clone its structure** across the other trades + job-types. Keep a swipe file. Zombie-relaunch the good-but-starved ones. |
| **4** | **Don't make ads look like ads** | People hate ads. Run content that already performs *organically*. He runs a 46-min raw YouTube vid (millions of organic views) AS an ad. Burner-account trick: follow niche pages, see what rises, make that. | **Real curved-job content, clean.** No wordmark lockups, no CTA pills, no catalogue panels. If it looks designed, it's dead. The photo is the content. |
| **5** | **Broad targeting + super-specific creative** | The creative is the targeting. **Long-form copy** gives Meta a bigger context window to find buyers. Short basic ads = tiny context = limited reach. Stop interest-stacking; go broad. | **Broad AU, empty interests.** **Long-form, specific body copy** packed with the trade + job-types + the real pain. The copy targets. |
| **6** | **Match ad → landing page (scent)** | Headlines get seen ~1000× more than the page. Run 20-40 headlines, mirror the winner on the page (H1, sub-head, lead-in) → +15-20% conversion. Keep ≥3 split tests live. | Run the headline variants below; **mirror the winner on the top of `radius-online`**. A launch-checklist item. |
| **7** | **Retarget with a different offer** | They didn't buy because *that* offer wasn't right. Retarget with other products. Full funnel: TOF ad → page → high-value content offer → call every lead → objection-handling ads + proof/testimonial carousels + other-offers CBO + a value "audit". | Bounced off Radius Pro → retarget with **Formwork Builder / Architrave Builder / a curved-jobs field guide**. (Phase 2. After the TOF test reads.) |
| **8** | **Track net free cash flow, not ROAS %** | Don't optimise ROAS %. Optimise blended ROAS at the business level → really, net free cash. Find break-even, scale hard toward it. 3 hrs/month eyeballing your own numbers. | **Net cash is the score.** ~$1,450 avg curved job. A $30-60 lead that lands those is a scale signal, not a ROAS % to protect. |

**The through-line:** feed the algorithm real, native creative (statics, volume), let long-form
trade-specific copy do the targeting (broad AU), match the winning headline to the page, retarget
with our other products, and judge it all on net cash. We already own the raw material. The fuel is
**real curved-job photos**, not AI stand-ins.
