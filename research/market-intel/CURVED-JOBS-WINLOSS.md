# Curved Jobs — Win/Loss & Demand Intel (from ClickUp)

_First-party demand intelligence. Source: **ClickUp → CNC Cut → Job List**, curved-work jobs, pulled
2026-07-21. This is real Craftons/CNC Cut demand — far higher-signal than scraped forum quotes.
Companion: `Craftons-Curved-Jobs-WinLoss.xlsx` (the full 68-row table + summary)._

> **Status → outcome mapping** (Job List pipeline): `complete` / in-production (`delivery`,
> `manufacture`, `pickup`, `design`, `material order`, `deposit invoice`) = **WON**; `awaiting approval`
> = **quote sent, not proceeded (stalled/lost)**; `quote` / `new orders` = open; `decline to quote` = we
> passed. Product tags are best-effort from task names (not a full read of every job); a few
> formwork/concrete jobs are inferred from company + "formply/formwork" naming.

## The headline

**Curved work is a major, recurring revenue line — ~68 jobs — and conversion varies sharply by product.**

| Product | Won | Quote stalled | Open | Win rate* |
|---------|----:|----:|----:|----:|
| **Radius Pro** (curved wall plates / ply curves / MDF) | 24 | 9 | 0 | **73%** |
| **Formwork / Concrete** (formply curves, curved formwork, curved seats) | 14 | 14 | 2 | **50%** |
| **Curved Architraves** (arch, window curve, architrave) | 3 | 0 | 2 | (n too small) |
| **Total** | **41** | **23** | **4** | **64%** |

_*Won / (Won + stalled), excluding still-open quotes._

## What it tells us

1. **Radius Pro (curved wall plates) is the proven workhorse.** Highest volume *and* highest win rate
   (73%). Builders repeatedly order curved top/bottom wall plates cut to their set-out. **Repeat buyers =
   the 20%:** Perfetto Homes, Invoke Construction, Glenvill, LBA Construction, Frameworks, Montegar,
   Marc Cotsis. This product already works — the marketing job is *more of the same buyer*.

2. **Curved formwork/concrete is the big opportunity — half the quotes stall.** Strong demand (concreters,
   formworkers, landscapers — Concretum, Top Form, Policrete, Northpoint, Highend, Butterworth, McCosker,
   Formworx, Mccoll Smith…) but only ~50% convert. That's the **DIY-vs-buy decision** in action — it maps
   exactly to the scraped voice (*"$20k, hope I don't get it," "massive slog"*): they quote it, then decide
   to grind it out themselves. **This is precisely where the Godfather offer + risk reversal should aim** —
   lift formwork conversion from 50% and it's a large revenue unlock.

3. **Curved architraves is a real product gap, not just a research gap.** Only a handful of jobs ever.
   Demand exists (Fiander, De Ree, Tbuild arch, SK window curve) but it's not being pursued. Decide:
   invest to grow it, or de-prioritise vs the two proven lines.

4. **The stalled pile is a warm re-engagement list.** ~23 companies quoted curved work and didn't proceed
   — a ready-made outreach/retargeting list (esp. the formwork/concrete names).
