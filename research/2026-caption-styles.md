# Reel caption styles — what the best accounts do, and the top 5 for Craftons

_Research 2026-09-04 after reel 01. Web sources at the bottom; brand examples marked (obs) are from observed
practice, not from those sources. Companion to `pipeline/video/REEL-PROCESS.md` and `SOCIAL-VOICE.md`._

## Why captions matter (the numbers, so we stop debating it)
- **Most Reels are watched without sound.** Figures range from ~60% to 85% sound-off depending on the source.
- Captioned Reels hold viewers **15–40% longer**; completion ~65% vs ~37% uncaptioned (Zebracat/Upgrow roundups).
- The style that dominates TikTok/Reels/Shorts in 2026 is **word-by-word or short-phrase captions, high contrast,
  lower-middle third, synced to speech** (OpusClip, Blitzcut, Kapwing, Digital Zoom Studio all agree).
- **Custom captions in brand font + colour are the tell** between accounts that look "built" and ones that look
  "assembled" (Digital Zoom Studio). Generic CapCut yellow is the assembled look.
- Technical floor: ≥36 pt body / 48 pt+ headline at 1080 wide, ≥2 s per text element, keep to the centre 80%
  of the frame, never light-on-light.

## The taxonomy (everything we saw, one line each)
| Style | What it is | Who does it (obs) | Verdict for Craftons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Karaoke / word highlight | Text in short blocks, the spoken word changes colour or gets a box | Hormozi (yellow), most creator-led brands | **Have it.** Ours is green-on-green, no box. |
| Hook title card | 5–8 word full-frame claim in the first 1–2 s, then captions | Almost every high-completion how-to | **Add.** Cheap, biggest lift per minute of work. |
| List / step overlay | "1 of 6" counters or numbered lines that appear as you go | How-to and "3 things" formats | **Add for How-To lane.** Drives completion. |
| Editorial lower-third | Small sentence-level caption, left-aligned, brand rule/label, no animation | Economist, Vox, FT; premium DTC (July, Bellroy, Gozney) | **Add for Craft Macro / premium lane.** Restraint = premium. |
| UI callout / annotation | Text tied to the thing on screen: pill + arrow on the button, field, price | Software product reels (Notion, Figma, Linear) | **Add for screen demos.** Says "click here" without narrating. |
| Text-behind-subject / knockout | Big word behind the person, or video showing through the letters | Fashion, fitness, personal brands | **Opener only.** One hero word on the motif. |
| Solid box captions | Whole line in a filled box | Newsy/explainer accounts | We tried it; heavy. Keep for busy backgrounds only. |
| Bouncing / pop / shake text | Every word animates in | Meme accounts, CapCut defaults | **No.** Reads amateur; off the calm-confident voice. |
| Emoji-decorated captions | Emoji as punctuation | Consumer brands | **No** (locked in `SOCIAL-VOICE.md`). |
| ALL CAPS captions | Shouty transcript | Hormozi imitators | **No** — caps are for compliance stamps only (`BRAND.md`). |

## Top 5 for engagement, ranked for us
1. **Karaoke highlight, brand-coloured (the base layer).** The data-backed default. Ours: Inter Bold 84 px,
   Craftons green, 2×2 stacked, spoken word in line green, no box, bottom at y=1190. Keep it on every talking
   reel. The only change worth testing: the live word in **ink black** instead of line green for a harder flick.
2. **Hook title card (0–2 s) + step counter.** A full-frame line in the first two seconds
   (*"Curves are on every plan now."*) in Aeonik/Inter on the green motif panel, then a small **"1 / 6"** in the
   top-left that ticks over as the walkthrough moves. Costs one more ASS layer and a title still. Lifts both the
   hook and completion, which are the two Reels ranking signals we can influence.
3. **UI callouts on the screen recording.** Instead of only transcribing the voice, put a green pill with a short
   label and a 2 px leader line on the element being used: *Material*, *External dims*, *Add straight legs*,
   *Save & share*. This is what software brands do and it is the single cleverest thing we can do that a
   building-products competitor won't. It also carries the demo for sound-off viewers better than a transcript.
4. **Editorial lower-third.** For Craft Macro and Built-with-Craftons posts where nobody is talking to camera:
   a single sentence, small (48–56 px), left-aligned, Inter Medium, a 4 px green rule to its left, bottom-left,
   held for the shot. Premium restraint; brand barely there (which is the lane rule).
5. **Knockout / text-behind opener.** One hero word (*Curves.*) behind the presenter or with the CNC footage
   showing through the letters, 1–2 s, then cut to the walkthrough. Use sparingly — once per video, never as
   running captions.
