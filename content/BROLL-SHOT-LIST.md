# B-roll shot list — Fairfield

For script 01, plus a bank worth grabbing while the camera's already out.

## Rules

- **Vertical.** 9:16, the whole time
- **Roll 10 seconds minimum per shot.** You need 2 seconds; the other 8 are the ones you'll actually use
- **Move half as fast as feels right.** Slow moves cut well, quick ones don't
- **Brace the phone.** A bag of sand, a sheet on edge, anything. Handheld drift ruins slow shots
- **Shoot everything twice**, different height or angle
- **Step closer, never zoom**
- **Wipe the part first.** Dust reads as neglect on camera, and the part in shot is the product shot

## The five for this video

| # | Shot | Frame | Roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Plate edge** | Tight enough that you can't tell what it is at first. Ply laminations and the film face filling frame. Track slowly *along* the curve | 15s |
| 2 | **Stack of plates** | Wider, workshop behind, machine in shot if you can. This is the last frame of the video so it has to look like a business | 15s |
| 3 | **Set-out drawing** | Phone straight down, flat, hand resting on the paper. Pick one that's a curve with a radius on it and not much else | 10s |
| 4 | **You at the bench** | Medium, standing, machine behind, natural light. Three takes of the whole script | — |
| 5 | **Screen recording** | Full window at 1440 wide. Shape row → radius → the arc/chord readout → the split → the price. Four separate recordings, not one | — |

Shots 1, 2 and 3 are the ones that make or break it. If the light's bad on 2, move the stack rather than accept it.

## Worth banking while you're there

Costs you ten minutes and it's what stops the next video being blocked.

- **The engraved part ID**, macro, as close as the phone will focus
- **Plates on the conveyor**, top-down if you can get above it
- **A hand lifting a plate** off the belt or off the stack
- **Formply sheet stack**, edge on
- **The nest skeleton** — the sheet after the parts are out, if that exists
- **The machine cutting**, spindle down, dust extraction running
- **A bundle strapped up** ready to go
- **Anything finished and curved**, best-looking thing in the shop

The first two unlock the part-ID video, which is next. The machine shot is the right payoff for a
Radius Pro video and you'll want it eventually.
