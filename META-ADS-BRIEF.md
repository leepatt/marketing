## 4. The pain to lead with (make them feel seen first)

Curved work is the **dreaded, slow, uncertain** part of the job. Lead the ad with the pain, THEN the
product as the escape:
- It eats a day and still isn't right; "cut perfectly" is the worry.
- They can't easily find a supplier who does curves online.
- Concreters can build formwork themselves — so we must beat their own method on **speed + finish + no-slog**.
- The #1 real friction in the old way: waiting on a manual quote. **The builders kill that with an instant price.**

## 6. Meta static-ad rules (from the bible §9 — the 8-hacks playbook)

1. **Statics on volume.** Meta's Andromeda AI eats fresh creative — make *many* statics, cheaply. Aim for
   a batch, not one hero. (Craft Macro stills + configurator renders + spec/number cards = infinite source.)
2. **Identity-keyword hack — the creative IS the targeting.** Put the trade IN the ad. Make per-niche
   variants: **"Concreters —"**, **"Builders —"**, **"Formworkers —"**, plus job-types (curved bench seat,
   curved stairs, arched doorway). Each opens a new audience + drops CPL.
3. **Don't look like an ad — go native.** Make statics feel like organic build/site content (real jobs,
   real parts, a number, a curve), not a catalogue card. If it reads as an ad, it's dead on arrival.
4. **Broad targeting + specific creative.** The copy targets; run broad AU.
5. **Ad ↔ landing-page match.** The static's headline must match the top of the builder page it points to
   (scent). Winning headline → mirror on the page.
6. **Judge on net cash, not ROAS %.**

**Formats to produce per concept:** 4:5 feed (1080×1350) primary + 1:1 (1080×1080) + 9:16 story (1080×1920).
Keep on-image text short and legible; safe-zone the story (top ~250px / bottom ~340px clear of UI).
Meta text fields: **primary text** (~125 chars before "…more"), **headline** (~27–40 chars), optional description.

## 7. Brand voice (for the words on the ad)

- **Builder-to-builder.** Written by someone who's been on site. Explain *why it matters*, assume they know
  the terms (formply, NCC, radius, set-out).
- **Confident, not boastful.** "We help you build better," never "world-class solutions."
- **Action-first headlines, period-terminated:** *Build Now. Cut to size. Priced online.*
- **No emoji. No jargon** (no "synergy/leverage/seamless/game-changing"). Trade language only.
- **"We" = Craftons, "you" = the builder.** Never "the customer." Testimonials = first name + last initial.
- A little dry Aussie humour + light slang (mate, chippy, bog-and-sand, sorted) — sprinkled, never forced.
- **Casing:** sentence/title case; **ALL CAPS only for spec/compliance callouts** (e.g. *F17 STRUCTURAL*,
  *CUT TO YOUR RADIUS*) paired with a condensed/stencil face.

## 8. Brand look (for the visual design)

- **Colours:** deep forest green `#194431` (signature), darker `#123022`, darkest `#0a1c14`; bright line
  green `#2d8a5b` (curve motif on dark); black `#000000`; near-black ink `#0e0e0c`; warm off-white/paper
  neutrals (no blue cast). No gradients on surfaces.
- **Type:** Display = **Aeonik** (geometric grotesk; the only display face). Body = **Inter**. Condensed/
  stencil for spec stamps = **Big Shoulders Display / Anton**. Mono numbers = **JetBrains Mono**.
  Display-heavy hierarchy — builders read fast, go big.
- **Signature motif:** the **curved-line pattern** (wood-grain abstracted into a Lissajous curve) — use it
  **big, once, behind the hero text.** Green-on-black or green-on-green. This is the brand's device.
- **Photography:** product-on-white (catalogue) OR warm, real, unstaged tradies-on-site. Real footage/parts
  lead; **"big company, never small workshop"** — studio-clean or rendered, never phone-in-the-shed.
- **Devices:** the all-caps pipe-separated **marquee strip**; **spec / compliance stamps** on patterned
  green panels. Buttons ~6px radius, dark green.
- **Tagline:** *Building Smarter Starts Here.* Assets (logo, motif PNGs, Aeonik fonts) live in Drive
  `…/01 Craftons/Marketing/assets/` + `fonts/`. Full brand skill: `.claude/skills/craftons-design/`.

## 9. Static-ad concept starters (to build from)

- **Pain-first / native:** a real curved job photo + a plain-text overlay in their words —
  *"A curved wall's a good day gone. Or a five-minute order."* → logo + "Radius Pro".
- **Spec-stamp:** a clean off-form curve + a compliance-style stamp — *"CUT TO YOUR RADIUS · F17 FORMPLY ·
  READY TO POUR."*
- **Identity/niche:** same layout, swap the trade word — *"Concreters: curved formwork, priced by the l/m."*
- **Number card:** the configurator render + one number — *"1300mm radius. Cut to size. Priced online."*
- **Before/after or "the reveal":** the form strip → flawless curve (Craft Macro), text: *"Perfect curve. No slog."*
- **Question hook (their real question):** *"What ply gets a clean off-form finish? That's our call."* → Formwork Builder.

Always: pain/hook first → the product as the escape → the builder as the CTA ("Design it online"). One idea per ad.
