# Script 01 — Formwork Builder

**Lee's words.** ~45 seconds, about 600 characters of speech.

Built from Lee's own dictation and the real enquiry language in
`research/market-intel/VOICE-OF-CUSTOMER-curved-jobs.md`. The Plot structure sits underneath
it — problem, then the thing, then the reason, then close on cost and programme — but there is
no framework showing and nothing left blank.

---

## The script

> "Since we launched the Radius Pro we've had concreters ringing us for something we didn't make.
>
> They want the whole lot. Top and bottom plates, face ply, cut to the set-out, bundled up and sent
> to site. And to get a price they'd send us a drawing and wait a few days.
>
> So we built the Formwork Builder. You put your shape and your inner radius in, and it gives you
> your arc and your chord as you go, then splits the curve into plates that fit a sheet.
>
> And the reason every plate comes out with its own ID cut into it is that a curved wall can be
> twenty-odd parts and no two of them are the same. So when it turns up, nobody's standing there
> guessing.
>
> It's priced when you configure it, so there's no variation later. Five days from checkout."

**Stop there.** No sign-off, no logo, no "link in bio". Across 148 Plot posts there isn't one.

**Caption:** Every plate knows where it goes.

---

## What's on screen

| While you say | Show |
|---|---|
| "concreters ringing us…" | Finished curved formwork or a stack of cut plates. **Not your face yet** |
| "top and bottom plates, face ply…" | The plates. Close |
| "So we built the Formwork Builder" | **You.** First time we see you. Bench, workshop behind |
| "shape and your inner radius" | The screen. Wall Shape row, then Inner Radius |
| "arc and your chord" | Tight on the readout — `3770mm arc · 2400mm chord` |
| "its own ID cut into it" | The engraved ID on a plate, on the belt |
| "no variation later. Five days" | Back to you, or the finished curve |

**Why you don't open on your face:** none of Plot's top performers do. Their presenter arrives about
a third of the way in, on the line that turns the problem into the solution. Yours lands exactly
there — "so we built the Formwork Builder".

---

## Two checks before you shoot

- **"Five days from checkout"** is off the product page. Confirm it's still true
- **The part ID** — is it cut in the same operation as the profile? If it's a separate step, say it
  differently

## One optional addition

If Jake can tell you what concreters actually try before they ring us — kerfing the ply, laminating
it up on site, cutting segments by hand, and why each one goes wrong — one line of that dropped in
after "wait a few days" is the strongest thing you could add. On Plot's data the dead ends are what
build the trust.

Not writing it myself. A made-up technical dead end gets spotted instantly and costs more than the
video earns.
