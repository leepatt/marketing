# Filming + editing economics — Tia's rates vs Jade's retainer

**Date:** 2026-09-12. Supports `research/jade-vs-market-comparison.md`.
**Tia's rates (from Lee):** **$100/hr + travel** to shoot · **$200 per video** to edit.
**Travel rate unknown** — modelled at ~$125/shoot (roughly 1–1.5 hrs round trip in Melbourne). Confirm with Tia.

---

## 1. One shoot per week, three scenarios

A "finished product" shoot = travel to a completed job, film the curved work in place.
1 shoot/week = **4.33 shoots/month**, 52/year.

| | Lean | Realistic | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hours on site | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Videos out per shoot | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| Shoot fee | $200 | $300 | $400 |
| Travel (est.) | $125 | $125 | $125 |
| Edit fee | $200 | $600 | $1,000 |
| **Per shoot** | **$525** | **$1,025** | **$1,525** |
| **Per month** | **$2,273** | **$4,438** | **$6,603** |
| **Per year** | **$27,300** | **$53,300** | **$79,300** |
| **Videos/month** | 4.3 | 13 | 21.7 |
| **Cost per video** | $121 | $79 | $70 |

Note the third column: **$6,603/month for ~22 videos, filming included** — versus Jade at
$7,000/month for unlimited editing and **no filming**.

---

## 2. The break-even that decides it

Jade's editing is only cheaper than Tia's once you pass this many videos a month:

| Jade's rate | Break-even vs Tia @ $200/video |
|---|---|
| $7,000/mo (intro) | **35 videos/month** |
| $12,000/mo (month 4+) | **60 videos/month** |

For reference:
- **Plot Joinery: 12–20 posts/week = 52–87/month.** Comfortably past break-even at both rates.
- **Craftons at 1 shoot/week: 4–22/month.** Below break-even at $7k in every scenario, and
  a long way below it at $12k.

**So on editing alone, Jade only becomes the cheaper option at roughly 3–8× the volume
Craftons is currently set up to produce.** The retainer is priced for Plot's output, not Craftons'.

---

## 3. The stacking problem

Jade does not film. So the real monthly cost of the Jade route is Jade **plus** a shooter:

| Route | Filming | Editing | Monthly | Videos/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tia films + Tia edits** | Tia | Tia | **$4,438** | ~13 |
| **Tia films + AU freelance editor** | Tia | freelance | **$3,040–4,240** | ~13 |
| **Tia films + Jade edits** | Tia ($1,840 shoot-only) | Jade | **$8,840** | unlimited ceiling |
| **Tia films + Jade edits, month 4+** | Tia ($1,840) | Jade | **$13,840** | unlimited ceiling |

Tia shoot-only = 3 hrs + travel × 4.33 = $1,840/month.

**Jade + Tia is roughly 2–3× the cost of Tia + a freelance editor for the same realistic volume.**
The gap only closes if Craftons gets to Plot-like output.

---

## 4. The volume premise breaks the 90-day programme

Jade's "first 30 posts are for collecting data" assumes Plot's cadence.

| Craftons output | Time to reach 30 posts |
|---|---|
| 4.3/month (lean) | **7 months** |
| 13/month (realistic) | **2.3 months** |
| 21.7/month (heavy) | 1.4 months |
| Plot's 52–87/month | 2 weeks |

At the lean end, the data-collection phase alone outlasts the entire three-month commitment.
**The programme's structure presumes a volume Craftons is not currently set up to produce** —
and the commitment is paid regardless.

---

## 5. What this does NOT price

Fairness: the comparison above is editing-and-filming only. Jade's retainer also includes
weekly 1:1 coaching, 24/7 access, strategy and the workflow build. Tia is a camera and an
edit suite, not a strategist. Those are different products — the point is that Craftons is
being asked to pay a volume-editing price for a coaching service.
