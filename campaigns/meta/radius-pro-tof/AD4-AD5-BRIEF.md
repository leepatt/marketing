# AD4 / AD5 Brief. Curved Wall (Builders + Carpenters)

> What we are trying to do, in one place. The build detail lives in `CURVED-WALL-BUILD-SPEC.md`; the
> copy lives in `AD-CONCEPTS.md`; the method that governs all of it is `../../../playbooks/
> META-ADS-SABRI-8-HACKS.md` (and its transcript). This file is the "why and what". Read it first.

## The goal

Add ads 4 and 5 to the live Radius Pro top-of-funnel Meta set, aimed at a new audience: **builders and
carpenters**, on the strength of a **curved timber-framed wall**. Strictly Radius Pro (curved wall
plates cut to the radius). Not bench seats, not architraves.

This is Sabri hack 2/3 in action: one proven campaign structure, cloned to a new identity trigger by
swapping the trade word. AD4 speaks to **builders**, AD5 to **carpenters / chippies**. Same creative,
one word changed.

## The problem we are solving

We have **no real photo** of a curved wall being framed with our Formply plates. Doctrine says real
footage leads and AI only extends when nothing real exists (hack 4: native, real-looking, never
"ad-looking"). This is that exception, so we are **AI-generating** the creative, built from Lee's real
reference photos and to Lee's exact construction spec, so it still reads as a genuine job, not an ad.

## The creative idea (the reveal)

A **before/after, same-viewpoint reveal**:
- **Slide 1, finished:** the finished curved feature wall (reworked from Lee's client photo `IMG_5539`
  so it becomes our own image, not the client's literal photo).
- **Slide 2, frame:** the **same curved wall from the same viewpoint**, shown as bare timber frame
  mid-construction. Swipe from finished to frame = "here is the beautiful feature, here is how it was
  actually built, and the curved bit turned up cut to the radius".

The frame slide is the hard part and the current focus. The finished slide and the full swipe pairing
are a later step (Lee: "just nail this image first").

Why this works for the trade: builders and chippies do not scroll past a real curved wall being framed.
It shows the exact moment our product matters (the curved top and bottom plates), and the finished shot
shows the payoff they are quoting for. The long-form caption does the targeting and funnels them to the
`radius-online` builder page. We are not selling in the ad, we are funnelling the right trades to the
page (the one idea the whole method rests on).

## What "done" looks like for the asset

The frame image matches the shape of `IMG_5539` (a convex curve joining two straight walls, bulging
toward the camera) AND is built exactly to `CURVED-WALL-BUILD-SPEC.md`:
- doubled 17mm Formply plates (34mm) top and bottom, black faces, ply-grain edges, flush 90mm
- 90x35 studs at 125mm centres, 35mm face to room, full height
- single slightly-imperfect noggin row at mid height
- concrete slab, exposed trusses, zero finished work

Current best render: `BEST_curved-wall-frame.png` (nano-banana, from `IMG_5539` + framing refs). Matches
the shape and build; open items are the grey backing behind the studs, the finished-looking room through
the left opening, and a 125-centre regen, then a clean 1080x1350 (4:5) export.

## The plan (in order)

1. **Lock the frame image** (this step). Nail shape + build per the spec, export 4:5 and 1:1.
2. **Rework the finished shot** from `IMG_5539` into our own image, matched to the frame viewpoint.
3. **Write AD4 + AD5 copy** off the locked question-hook structure (see `AD-CONCEPTS.md`), identity word
   the only change between them (builder vs chippy). Cut-not-bend, no em/en dashes.
4. **A human approves every asset** (house rule). Nothing goes live without Lee's sign-off.
5. **Wire AD4 + AD5** into the live ad set (`120247183658270186`), built PAUSED for a real Meta preview,
   then flip to active. Destination `radius-online`, UTMs `ad4_builders` / `ad5_carpenters`.
6. **Read on net cash** at the next campaign read, clone the winner further if it performs.
