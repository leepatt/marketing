## ⚠️ A conflict worth knowing about

The winning ad's own final line was **"Laminate two to 34mm and stand your wall."** The word
**"laminating" is banned** by Lee's product briefing (`radius-pro-product-truth.md`).

The banned word shipped in the best-performing ad in the account. The ban is later and specific, so it
stands — and the repo already carries the approved phrasing: **"double them up"** (batch ad #21, and
Lee's own words in product-truth: *"Some double them up — two at top, two at bottom"*). Every ad below
uses that. **The mechanic survives; only the word changes.**

**Banned throughout:** bog-and-sand · bogging · kerfing · kerf bending · curve bending ·
bendy-ply-as-the-problem · laminating · wiggle wood.
