Jade Ryuki has offered Craftons a 90-day content programme at $7,000/month + GST on a three-month commitment, reverting to $12,000/month thereafter. This is what that buys, and what the same outcome costs everywhere else in Melbourne in 2026.

01

The real number

ex GST

The proposal leads with $7,000. That is an introductory rate, and the decision in front of you is larger than one month.

Committed on signing $21,000 Three months at the intro rate. $23,100 inc GST.
Monthly, from month 4 $12,000 Confirmed by Jade. A 71% step-up. $13,200 inc GST.
Year one, if it continues $129,000 3 × $7k + 9 × $12k. $141,900 inc GST. This is the number to decide against.

For context you gave: $7,000/month would be more than all current Craftons marketing spend combined — and this programme carries no paid-ads component.

At $7,000 the offer sits at the top of the full-service agency band (G) — while being the only option on the board that excludes filming, commits to no volume, and doesn't touch paid ads. At $12,000 it costs the same as a full-time employee (J) who would film, work five days a week, and work only for Craftons.

The fair caveat: H and I are not the same product as F and G. Agencies rent you a service; Jade is selling capability transfer, weekly founder coaching and unlimited editing. Nobody in the $2–5k band gives an owner a weekly 1:1 and 24/7 text access. That difference is real, and the agency comparison does not price it.

04

Both sides

stated plainly

What's genuinely strong

Merit the price analysis doesn't capture

  • High-touch access is rare.Weekly 1:1s and 24/7 text from the person who did the work. Agencies at $3–5k give you an account manager and a monthly report.
  • The case study is adjacent and real.Melbourne, trade, selling to builders and architects. Far closer to Craftons than a generic social agency, in a genuinely underserved niche.
  • Unlimited editing is uncapped upside.If Jake reaches even 20 posts a month, the unit economics beat every other option on the board.
  • Capability transfer, not dependency.The stated goal is that Craftons owns the workflow after 90 days. Agencies are structured so you never leave.
  • It removes the real bottleneck."Never touch a timeline again." Editing is where owner-operator content dies.
  • No conflict of interest.Plot does architectural joinery; Craftons does curved building products. Adjacent audience, not a competitor.

Structural risks

Specific to this deal, not generic caution

  • He's moonlighting.Solo, still full-time at Plot. Craftons buys his evenings and weekends. Three spots at $7k is $21k/month of side income on top of a full-time job.
  • No backup.One person, no team. Illness, holiday or a busy month at his day job all land on Craftons with no redundancy.
  • His employer is also his largest client.Plot pays $12k, Craftons $7k. If attention is ever contested, the ranking isn't in your favour.
  • Craftons carries the filming, untested.Jake is new to camera and the workshop is at capacity. This is the single biggest unknown in the deal.
  • The Plot result has confounds.Charley Li was already Instagram-forward and has hosted the Site to Studio podcast since ~2020. The engine predates the 12-month window, the product is visually spectacular, and the founder is a natural on camera.
  • "Content-attributed quotes" is not revenue.Quotes issued ≠ jobs won ≠ margin. The attribution method was never stated.
  • The 140K+ is unreconciled.Public counts total roughly 81K — Instagram ~34K, Facebook ~39K, TikTok ~8K, YouTube unverified. May legitimately include Charley's personal account or be cross-platform reach. Worth a dated screenshot.
  • Phase 1 re-buys work you own.Content pillars, social voice, brand memory, swipe file and the Marketing Bible already lock pillars, voice, audience and angles.

06

Put to Jade

before anything is signed

None of these are hostile. They are the things a contract would normally pin down, and every one of them is absent from the proposal.

  1. Dated per-platform screenshots of Plot's growth — start versus now.
  2. How "content-attributed quotes" was actually measured.
  3. Hours per week Craftons gets, given the full-time Plot role.
  4. A minimum monthly deliverable volume, in writing.
  5. Confirmation in writing that Craftons films — and what gear he expects.
  6. Turnaround time per video.
  7. What happens in a month where Craftons films very little.
  8. Whether scripting, captions, posting and community management are in or out.
  9. Cover during illness or holiday.
  10. Who owns the workflow docs, strategy and footage after 90 days.
  11. Payment terms, and whether month 4 at $12k is negotiable or committed.
  12. Who the other two spots are, and whether any is a competitor.
