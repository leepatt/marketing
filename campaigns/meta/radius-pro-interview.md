# Radius Pro — product interview

_Questions for Lee (and whoever runs the machines). Written 2026-08-03._

**Why this exists:** I wrote six live-ready ads containing product claims I had never verified with
anyone who makes the product. Three of them are wrong or misleading. This is the fix, and it should
happen before any copy is rewritten.

**How to use it:** voice-note the answers if that's faster — Vibe/Whisper output is fine, I'll distil
it. Rough answers beat polished ones. **"I don't know" and "it depends" are useful answers** — they
tell me what a claim can't say.

> ⚠️ Anything not answered here **cannot appear in an ad.** That's the rule that was broken.

---

## 1. The claims I already made and cannot currently stand behind 🔴

These are in rendered ads right now. Each needs a yes/no/qualified.

1. **"Dispatched in three business days."** True? For every material and size, or only some? What
   pushes it out — big splits, unusual materials, volume? Is it a promise or a typical?
2. **"On site in three days."** I wrote this as a headline while the sub-line said *dispatched*.
   What's the realistic **delivered** time for Melbourne metro? What should the ad actually say?
3. **"Part IDs engraved."** Are they genuinely engraved (cut into the face) or printed/labelled?
   On every split part, or only some? What do they look like?
4. **"Nothing to fill at the join."** I think this is **wrong** — large curves auto-split into
   sections, so there *are* joins. What does a customer actually have to do at a join? Is it a
   butt joint, a scarf, a biscuit? Does it need filling or not?
5. **Bending ply.** Do you *sell* it? My ad said *"bendy ply is the slow way round"* — which may
   attack your own product, and "bendy ply" is a **confirmed job-winning search term**. Is bending
   ply a competitor to Radius Pro, a complement, or a product line?

## 2. What Radius Pro actually is

6. Describe it as you'd describe it to a chippie on site who's never heard of it. Two sentences.
7. What **materials**, and which is most ordered?
8. What's the **smallest** and **largest** radius you can cut? Thickest material?
9. What are the **real constraints** a customer hits? (Sheet size, max part length, minimum width,
   tolerances.)
10. What does the customer **receive** — flat parts, a kit, assembled, packaging?
11. What do they still have to **do themselves** once it arrives?

---

## 3. The split behaviour (the strongest differentiator, and the one I got wrong)

12. When does a part get split? Purely arc length vs sheet, or other reasons?
13. How are split parts **joined** on site? What's the join detail?
14. Is the split a **feature** (it arrives ready to assemble) or a **constraint** (it's a compromise)?
    The app shows it as an amber warning; my ad framed it as a feature. Which is honest?
15. What's the **biggest** thing you've cut this way?

---

## 4. The buyer — replacing my guesses with your knowledge

16. Who actually orders Radius Pro? Trades, ratio, repeat vs one-off?
17. **The 20% rule:** which customers or job types drive most of the profit?
18. What do they say when they **first call or email**? Their words, not a summary. This is the
    single most valuable thing in this document.
19. What do they get **wrong** or ask about most?
20. What makes someone **not** buy — price, doubt it'll fit, don't understand it, wrong product?
21. What did they do **before** Craftons? Which method are we actually replacing?

---

## 5. The fear (the doctrine says this is the whole game)

Drive `MARKETING-BIBLE.md`: *"his real fear isn't price; it's the curve not fitting on site and
blowing the program in front of the builder."*

22. Is that right, in your experience? If not, what is the real fear?
23. Has a Craftons part ever **not fitted**? What happened, and what did you do?
24. What do you tell a nervous first-time customer to reassure them?

---

## 6. The offer (gates all copy — Drive bible §4)

Draft: *"Send us your set-out. We'll CNC-cut your curved parts, label every piece, and deliver them
ready to fix. If a single part doesn't seat to your line, we re-cut it free and cover the freight."*

25. Can you actually stand behind that? What would you change?
26. Where's the boundary between **our error** and a **bad set-out from the customer**?
27. What's the freight exposure, and does it need a cap?
28. Public now, or piloted quietly first?

---

## 7. Proof

29. Any jobs you can name, or photos of finished work? (This unblocks the **before/after** family —
    still the strongest angle and the only one that can't be assembled from existing assets.)
30. Any customer quotes, reviews or texts I can use **verbatim**? First name + last initial per the
    brand rules.
31. Anything you're proud of that we're not saying?
