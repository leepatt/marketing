02 / The set-out numbers
Chord and rise. That's your set-out.
Chord is where the plate starts and ends.
Rise is how far it bows off the string line.
String the chord, mark the rise at the middle. Nobody is swinging a 3.7 metre trammel on a slab.
chord = 2R sin(θ/2)   ·   rise = R(1 − cos θ/2)
arc = Rθ   ·   θ in radians, 30° = 0.5236
