# Keyword Plan — Craftons

_Created 2026-06-30 via `/keyword-research`. Goal: **leads & sales.** Weighting: **both** quick wins
(short-term leads) + long-term authority pillars. Feeds `seo-content` (articles) AND the AdWords
campaign (paid). Source context: `brand/audience.md`, `brand/competitors.md`._

> **Data quality:** SERP/competition validated via web search (US-biased engine, so AU volumes are
> directional, not exact). Volumes shown as qualitative est. (High/Med/Low). Validate exact AU
> volumes in Google Keyword Planner once the Ads account is live.
>

> terms that have won jobs — prioritised in ads + page titles below.

## Executive summary

### Top opportunities
1. **Bending / curved plywood** — real buyer demand (a dozen AU suppliers competing), but content is
   thin supplier pages. Craftons *manufactures* it + owns the curved specialty → can out-teach and out-rank.
2. **"How to build curved / off-form" (the value lane)** — SERP is US sites + YouTube; **Australian
   practitioner gap.** Craftons has real builds (How-To Series) + the Formwork Builder. White space.
3. **Formply / formwork plywood** — supplier-blog territory (Danterr, ATS, Plysupply), winnable with
   genuinely useful, AU-specific content; strong commercial intent.

### Quick wins (3-month, lead-driving)
- **"bendy ply" ⭐** — confirmed converter (jobs won); low competition, high intent. Lead with it.
- **"curved bench seat" ⭐ / "curved bench seat formwork"** — confirmed search; the Formwork Builder's hero use case.
- **"curved architrave"** — low-competition niche, high intent (Architrave Builder owns this fast).
- **"minimum bending radius" / "how tight can plywood bend"** — specific, low competition, high intent.
- **"what is formply" / "formply grades (F17)"** — commercial, thin competition.
- **"how to set out a curved wall"** — how-to, AU gap, converts to leads + feeds the How-To Series.
- **"acoustic perforated plywood panels"** — architect commercial intent; a proven seller.

### Long-term plays (6–12 months, authority)
- **"curved plywood"** (broad head term) — pillar guide, build authority over time.
- **"off-form concrete"** — competitive/technical SERP; architect-facing pillar.
- **"formwork for curved walls / curved formwork"** — ties directly to the Formwork Builder.

### Start here
**Pillar guide: "Bending Plywood — the complete AU guide (radius, uses, how to work it)."** It's the
intersection of buyer intent + Craftons' proprietary advantage + winnable competition. Anchors the
whole bending-ply cluster and gives the AdWords landing page real depth.
